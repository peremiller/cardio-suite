Clinical Triage & Symptom Escalation Protocol

When handling incoming phone calls or patient inquiries, immediately triage red-flag cardiovascular symptoms.

🚨 RED FLAG (Immediate 911 / Emergent)

  • Active, crushing chest pressure or pain
  • Acute dyspnea / severe breathlessness at rest
  • Syncope (fainting) or acute altered mental status
  • Pain radiating to jaw, neck, or left arm

⚠️ URGENT (Same-Day Physician Notification)

  • New onset peripheral edema (swelling in legs)
  • Weight gain > 3 lbs in 24 hrs (HF indicator)
  • Symptomatic bradycardia or palpitations
  • BP > 180/110 without acute symptoms

✅ ROUTINE (Standard Processing)

  • Prescription refill requests without symptom changes
  • Routine appointment rescheduling
  • Pre-procedure fasting questions
  • General lab result inquiry status
Cardiology Assistant Scope of Practice Boundary
Important Clinical Boundary: The Medical Assistant supports clinical workflows but must never independently diagnose medical conditions, interpret ECG/diagnostic results, alter medication doses, or give unapproved clinical advice.

Allowed Assistant Responsibilities

  • Performing 12-lead ECG lead placement
  • Hooking up Holter and Event monitors
  • Recording vital signs and weight accurately
  • Reconciling current medication lists
  • Transmitting diagnostic reports to the physician
  • Relaying physician-approved instructions to patients

Strictly Prohibited (Physician Scope Only)

  • Diagnosing arrhythmias or ECG wave changes
  • Informing patients of test results prior to provider review
  • Adjusting Anti-hypertensive or Anticoagulant doses
  • Providing medical opinions or reassurances on acuity
  • Ordering lab tests or diagnostics without authorization

⚠️ Scope of Practice Guard

Action Restricted: Medical Assistants are strictly prohibited from independently interpreting ECG tracings or providing clinical diagnostics.
